# MBA Entrance Practice · Nepal

Free interactive MCQ practice for MBA and management master's entrance exams in Nepal. Each university has its own bank, built to its own exam pattern, with chapter practice and full mock exams.

**▶ Start here: [newamacha.github.io/mba-entrance-practice](https://newamacha.github.io/mba-entrance-practice/)**

## How it works

- Pick a chapter and sub-chapter, or one of the 3 mock exams for your university.
- Tick an option: a green ✓ if it's right, or a red ✗ with the correct answer and a worked solution.
- A running score is kept for each block and mock; for SOMAT and KUMAT it shows the net score with negative marking.
- Progress is saved in your browser. Works on phones, no sign-in.

## Notes

- All questions are new practice questions written to each test's syllabus, not past papers.
- Exam patterns come from the universities' notices and bulletins where published, otherwise from exam listings. Always check the latest official notice before your exam.
